3 Commercial Lease Traps That Destroy SME Cashflow

TRAP #01 • REINSTATEMENT SHOCK

Inheriting Previous Tenant Demolition Costs (S$60k – S$140k)

Taking over an "as-is" fitted F&B or retail unit without a Schedule B Handover Exclusion Rider legally obligates you to demolish fixtures installed by the previous tenant when your lease ends.

TRAP #02 • UNCAPPED ANCILLARY HIKES

Unilateral Service Charge & Chilled Water Escalations

Even when base rent is fixed, uncapped Service Charge, A&P levy, and Chilled Water rate adjustments can silently increase your total occupancy cost by 12%–18% mid-term.

TRAP #03 • LOCKED EXIT CLAUSES

Zero Pre-Termination Exit When Anchor Tenants Leave

Without a properly structured FTIC pre-termination or capex amortization exit clause, closing an underperforming outlet can trigger full unexpired term rental liability.
