Dual-Entity Architecture: SGTUFF Institute (sgtuff.org)

While SGTUFF Co-operative Ltd (sgtuff.org.sg) serves Singapore tenants, SGTUFF Institute Ltd. (sgtuff.org) serves as the international non-profit policy, research, and grant-receiving arm using the Diplomatic Sister Palette (#0F172A Navy + #FAF7F2 Paper + #E8352A TUFF Red).
