# SGTUFF Brand Guide v2

Locked 23 Oct 2026 · Owner: Keson Lim · This file is the source of truth for the SGTUFF brand. Logo files: [`assets/brand/logo/`](assets/brand/logo/). Tokens: [`tokens/`](tokens/).

## Executive summary

v1 is a tidy collateral pack, not a brand system, and it points the wrong way. It dresses SGTUFF as a ministry: Cinzel serif, navy and gold, "statutory" language. The brief is modern, grassroots and tech-savvy, so v2 resets the direction rather than polishing v1.

**What v2 changes**

- **From "institution" to "movement with tools".** The new direction is Grassroots Modern: one loud signature colour, a characterful grotesk typeface, real shopkeeper photography and data-forward layouts.
- **One source of truth.** v1 has two primary reds, two navies and fonts that appear only in code. v2 has one palette, one type system and one set of logo files.
- **Claims you can defend.** Several v1 claims overstate SGTUFF's legal standing and partnerships. v2 rewrites them, and Section 10 lists what to verify before anything is printed.

**Decisions needed from you**

1. Approve the Grassroots Modern direction over v1's Direction A (Institutional).
2. Logo locked: the SGTUFF wordmark with the Unity U (Section 5).
3. Settled (updated 24 Oct 2026): one member-count baseline for all copy: "Representing 700+ frontline business owners, 600+ brands and 2,600+ outlets across Singapore (as of Oct 2026)."
4. Settled: Terence Yow is Chairman and holds the FTIC appointment; Keson Lim's card title is COM.

## 1. Audit of v1

v1 scores 4 out of 10 as a brand system. It is strong on assets (favicons, banners, print sizes) and weak on strategy, consistency and accuracy.

### 1.1 Logo audit

The emblem is a red double-stroke "U" with two dots above it. It is meant as the "U" in Unity; v1's write-up adds a story of two business owners linked in solidarity. At a glance it reads differently.

| Test | Result | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First read | Fail | Most people will see a horseshoe magnet, an umlaut "ü" or a smiley face before they see two people. |
| Meaning | Weak | The Unity "U" is a sound idea, but the double stroke and dots pull the eye to other readings first. Keep the idea; fix the drawing. |
| Distinctiveness | Weak | People-in-a-circle and linked-figure marks are the most common NGO and union cliché. |
| Name link | Missing | Nothing ties it to "SG" or "TUFF". The strongest asset, a name that sounds like "SG Tough", goes unused. |
| Scalability | Pass | It is simple and survives at 16px. This is its best quality. |
| System | Fail | There is no wordmark, horizontal lockup, one-colour or reversed version, and only a PNG file. |
| Execution | Mixed | The dark-mode version uses a red glow, which looks dated and will not print. |

**Verdict:** keep the Unity U, the dots and the red; fix the drawing. Section 5 sets out the final mark: the Unity U, redrawn and built into the wordmark.

### 1.4 Collateral review

- **Deck.** It leaks internal notes ("Direction A — Approved", local file paths, "Phase 1 Active"). It mixes a ministry briefing with a membership price list. Split it into an external briefing deck and a member pitch.
- **Letterhead.** The layout is solid. Fix the claims above and drop the partner-logo strip unless each partner has agreed.
- **Business cards.** Emoji icons will print badly. There is no QR code, which is the obvious tech-savvy touch.
- **Email signature.** The logo loads from a personal GitHub Pages address, so it will break if that repo changes. Host it on sgtuff.org.sg.
- **Web mockup.** The structure is good. The hero copy is long and the programme links go nowhere. There is no member login or tool, so the "tech-savvy" promise has nothing to point at.

## 2. Research: peers and style references

The groups that feel modern have moved away from navy, crests and protest clichés toward one bright ownable colour, plain-spoken copy and real people. v1 went the other way.

### 2.1 Peer organisations

| Organisation | What they are | Lesson for SGTUFF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Co-op (UK)](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/co-op-rebrands-to-old-logo-230516) | Large consumer co-op; rebranded with North in 2016 | It dropped a navy logo seen as too corporate for a people-focused co-op and moved to a lighter, brighter blue and a simple mark. The same critique applies to v1's navy. |
| [Independent Restaurant Coalition](https://www.independentrestaurantcoalition.com/) | US owner-led trade group formed in March 2020 | Same birth story as SGTUFF: a pandemic group of owners. It leads with one human line about independents not being alone, not with credentials. |
| [Freelancers Union](https://catherwood.library.cornell.edu/wit/labor-union-redefined-for-freelance-workers) | US union for independent workers | It recast the union model as benefits plus advocacy for a modern workforce. SGTUFF's shared-services offer is the same idea. |
| [London Renters Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Renters_Union) | Member-run tenants' union founded 2018, 7,000+ members | Grassroots credibility comes from visible member action and clear, specific campaigns. |
| [The Labor Force (case study)](https://liujason.cargo.site/the-labor-force) | A new labour organisation's identity | The designer deliberately avoided raised fists and protest imagery because they made the group look like every other one. |

### 2.2 Consumer-tech style references

| Brand | What to borrow | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monzo | One signature colour (hot coral) backed by deep navy and soft white. A friendly display face (Oldschool Grotesk) paired with a plain functional sans. A community that shapes the product. | [Monzo blog](https://monzo.com/blog/weve-had-a-little-makeover), [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/monzo-branding-ragged-edge/) |
| Wise | Chose green to escape the sea of fintech blue. Accessibility above minimum standards, many languages, and Inter as the secondary face. | [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/wise-rebrand-ragged-edge/) |
| Upwork | Warmth plus precision. Neue Montreal type, a wider palette of tones, and editorial photography of real freelancers rather than stock. | [Pangram Pangram](https://pangrampangram.com/blogs/font-in-use/upwork) |

### 2.3 What this means for SGTUFF

1. **Own one colour.** Pick a single hot red and use it with confidence. Drop gold and the navy-led look.
2. **Pair a character face with a workhorse face.** One display font with personality, one plain sans for everything else.
3. **Show the shopkeepers.** Real owners in their own shops, shot like a magazine, not stock handshakes.
4. **Lead with a human line, prove it with data.** Credentials go in the footer, not the headline.
5. **Make the tech real.** A member app, rent benchmarks and a lease checker are the brand. The website should point to tools, not describe them.
6. **Avoid movement clichés.** No raised fists, linked hands or people-in-a-circle.

## 3. Brand strategy

SGTUFF is the owner-run co-op that gives Singapore's independent shops and eateries the data, tools and collective voice to get a fair deal. It is pro-tenant without being anti-landlord, which matches the chairman's own public line that tenants failing hurts landlords too.

### 3.1 Purpose and positioning

- **Purpose:** keep Singapore's neighbourhood shops and eateries alive and thriving.
- **Positioning:** a movement with tools. Grassroots in who runs it, modern in how it works.
- **Member baseline (use verbatim):** "Representing 700+ frontline business owners, 600+ brands and 2,600+ outlets across Singapore (as of Oct 2026)."
- **Proof points:** owner-run since February 2020; a seat at the fair-tenancy table; shared services and lease help that save members real money.
- **What we are not:** a ministry, a law firm or a protest group.

### 3.2 Audiences

| Audience | What they need to feel | What we show them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner-operators (core) | "Someone has my back, and it's people like me." | Real members, plain help, quick wins on WhatsApp. |
| Next-gen founders | "This is modern and worth my time." | App, data tools, clean design, social-first content. |
| Landlords and REITs | "These are serious, reasonable partners." | Calm, evidence-led letters and benchmarks. |
| Government and media | "They speak for a real constituency." | Dated member numbers, case studies, clear asks. |

### 3.3 Personality

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Tough | Angry |
| Practical | Bureaucratic |
| Plain-spoken | Legalistic |
| Data-smart | Cold |
| Neighbourly | Cliquey |

### 3.4 Name and taglines

The name is the best asset in the brand. Say it "S-G-tough", and use the sound.

| Option | Use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tough together.** | Primary tagline (recommended) | Short, ownable and plays on the name. |
| Small shops. Strong voice. | Campaigns and recruitment | Says who and why in four words. |
| Fair rent is good business. | Landlord and policy audiences | Frames fairness as shared interest. |
| Unity is strength | Heritage line only | Keep as the founding motto in About pages; it is too common to lead with. |

Member programmes can carry the short form "TUFF", for example TUFF Benchmarks for rent data or TUFF Card for member perks.

## 4. Voice and tone

SGTUFF sounds like a sharp shop owner explaining things to a neighbour: short sentences, real numbers, no jargon. Tone flexes by audience; the voice does not.

### 4.1 Principles

1. **Say it like a person.** Write "we" and "you". Avoid "the co-operative hereby".
2. **Numbers beat adjectives.** "Rents rose 18% at renewal" beats "unprecedented pressure".
3. **Firm, never shrill.** State the problem and the ask. Skip exclamation marks and blame.
4. **Show, then claim.** Lead with a member's story or a data point; credentials come last.
5. **Short enough for WhatsApp.** If a message cannot be read on a phone in 10 seconds, cut it.
6. **Four languages ready.** Write simple English that translates cleanly into Chinese, Malay and Tamil.

### 4.2 Tone by audience

| Audience | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Members | Warm, direct | "Lease renewal coming up? Send us your offer letter. We'll benchmark it in 48 hours." |
| Prospects | Confident, inviting | "700+ owners already negotiate together. Join for $50." |
| Landlords | Calm, evidence-led | "Across 40 member renewals this quarter, the median increase was 18%. We'd like to discuss a fairer framework." |
| Government and media | Precise, constructive | "Three changes would protect established tenants without capping rents." |

The figures in these examples are placeholders; replace them with real data before use.

### 4.3 Rewrites from v1

| v1 | v2 |
| --- | --- |
| "SGTUFF is not a loose chat group; it is a registered Co-operative with statutory governance." | "We're a registered co-op, owned and run by our members." |
| "The Clout of Singapore's Frontline Tenancy" | "Who we speak for" |
| "A Respected Voice at the National Table" | "Where we have a seat, and what we've changed" |
| "Protecting & Empowering Singapore's Frontline Retail & F&B Tenants" | "Small shops. Strong voice." |

| --- | --- |

## 5. Logo

Locked on 23 Oct 2026: the SGTUFF wordmark with the Unity U built into it. The U is two halves that nearly meet at the base, under two dots: two members forming one union. Every version is drawn on the [SGTUFF logo canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/Di8NtG3Cx1FssmQAk4sHPD), boards W1 to W3.

### 5.1 The Unity U

- **Shape:** two strokes that nearly meet at the base, with a dot above each arm.
- **Proportions:** 15% wider and flatter than the first sketch, so the base gap stays visible down to app-icon size.
- **Meaning:** the U is Unity. Each half and its dot is a member; the gap shows members joining, not being swallowed into one.
- **Weight:** the stroke matches the stems of the wordmark letters.

### 5.2 The wordmark

- **Built from outlines.** Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold letters, with the Unity U in place of the letter U, fitted to letter height with the dots included.
- **Optical spacing.** The U sits tighter to the T, because the open space under the T's crossbar already reads as a gap. It sits wider from the first F, because two straight stems facing each other need more air. The gaps are unequal when measured so they look equal.
- **Joined FF.** The two Fs are tightened until they touch.
- **Never retype it.** Typing "SGTUFF" in a font will not reproduce the mark or its spacing. Always use the master files.

### 5.3 Colour combinations

| Name | Letters | U | Background | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | Ink | TUFF Red | Paper | Default: website, letters, documents, signage |
| **Night** | White SGTU, red FF | White | Ink | Social, events, merch |
| **Night Unity** | White | TUFF Red | Ink | Dark mode, member app, video end frames |
| **Day** | Ink SGTU, red FF | Ink | Paper | Light partner to Night: posters, packaging |
| **On Red** | White | Ink | TUFF Red | Campaign banners, booth backdrops |
| **Solid** | White | White | TUFF Red | Small sizes on red, embroidery, decals |
| **Marker** | Ink | Red Ink | Marker | Campaign highlights only |
| **Mono** | Ink | Ink | White | Single-colour print, stamps, forms |

Primary leads, so the red U carries the brand story. The red-FF versions (Night and Day) are for events and merch, not formal documents. On Marker the U uses Red Ink, because TUFF Red is too weak against yellow (3.0:1).

### 5.4 Lockups and app icon

| Lockup | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Horizontal: app icon + wordmark | Website header, letterhead, email signature |
| Stacked: app icon over wordmark + tagline | Social posts, posters, merch |
| Wordmark only | Inline mentions, co-branding, small print lines |
| App icon only | App, favicon, social avatars |
| Member decal: mark, wordmark and "Proud member" | Shop windows |

The app icon is the Unity U in Paper on a TUFF Red rounded square. Alternates: TUFF Red on Ink for dark settings, and TUFF Red on white.

### 5.5 Rules

- **Clear space:** half the wordmark's height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** wordmark 100px wide on screen and 30mm in print. Below that, use the app icon (16px and 6mm minimum).
- **Don't:** retype, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects; recolour outside Section 5.3; separate the dots from the U; place it on busy photos without a solid panel.
- **Files:** SVG masters for every combination plus the app icons are in [`assets/brand/logo/`](assets/brand/logo/); PNG icon sizes are in [`assets/brand/icons/`](assets/brand/icons/). Ask a designer for print-ready EPS and PDF masters before the first print run.

## 6. Colour

One hot red does the heavy lifting, on warm paper and near-black ink. Navy and gold are retired. Red stays because it is SGTUFF's existing equity and Singapore's colour; it becomes brighter and warmer so it reads as energy, not alarm.

### 6.1 Palette

| **TUFF Red** | Signature: logo, buttons, key moments | #E8352A | 232, 53, 42 | PMS Warm Red C |
| **Red Ink** | Red text and links on light backgrounds | #C1271D | 193, 39, 29 | PMS 485 C |

### 6.2 Proportions

Aim for roughly 70% Paper, 15% Ink, 10% TUFF Red and 5% for everything else. Red works because it is rare; a page that is all red shouts.

## 7. Typography

One characterful display face for headlines and one clean workhorse for everything else, the same pairing logic Monzo uses. All recommended fonts are free and open-licence, so members and volunteers can use them too.

### 7.1 Typefaces

| Role | Typeface | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display (headlines, big numbers) | **Bricolage Grotesque**, Bold and ExtraBold | Chunky and a little hand-made: grassroots energy with modern polish. | Google Fonts, free |
| Text (body, UI, documents) | **Inter**, Regular to Semibold | Highly legible on screens; used by Wise; huge language support. | Google Fonts, free |
| Data (tables, rent figures, code) | **JetBrains Mono** | Kept from v1; clear tabular numbers for benchmarks. | Google Fonts, free |
| Chinese | **Noto Sans SC** | Matches Inter's weight and tone. | Google Fonts, free |
| Tamil | **Noto Sans Tamil** | Same family logic. | Google Fonts, free |

Malay uses the Latin fonts above. Cinzel and Plus Jakarta Sans are retired.

### 7.2 Type scale (web)

| Style | Font | Size / line height | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Bricolage ExtraBold | 64 / 68px | Tracking -2%; one per page |
| H1 | Bricolage Bold | 44 / 48px | Tracking -1% |
| H2 | Bricolage Bold | 32 / 36px |  |
| H3 | Inter Semibold | 22 / 28px |  |
| Body | Inter Regular | 16 / 24px | Max line length about 70 characters |
| Small | Inter Regular | 14 / 20px |  |
| Label | Inter Semibold | 12 / 16px | Uppercase, tracking +8% |
| Data | JetBrains Mono | 14 / 20px | Tabular figures |

On mobile, scale Display to 40px and H1 to 32px.

### 7.3 Rules

- Sentence case for headlines. Capitals only for small labels.
- Highlight a key phrase with a Marker block behind it, not with bold red text.
- Big numbers get the display face: "18%" in Bricolage at full size, with a short label beneath.
- Never set body text in the display face.

## 8. Imagery, icons, layout and motion

Real owners in real shops, shot well, carry the grassroots half of the brand; clean data and UI carry the tech half.

### 8.1 Photography

- **Subjects:** members at work in their own shops, kopitiams, salons and stalls. Name them in captions.
- **Style:** natural light, eye level, slightly wide so the shop is part of the story. Warm grade, no heavy filters.
- **Avoid:** stock handshakes, empty malls, raised fists, people pointing at laptops.
- **Starter shoot:** 12 members, half a day each, with signed consent for reuse. This one library will feed the web, social and decks for a year.

### 8.2 Icons and illustration

- Use one open-source line icon set ([Lucide](https://lucide.dev) suits the type) at 1.5px stroke, in Ink or TUFF Red.
- No emoji in print or formal documents.
- Illustration is optional. If used, keep it flat, two colours (Ink and Red) and drawn from shop details: shutters, awnings, receipt rolls, keys.

### 8.3 Data style

- Benchmarks and survey results are a signature asset. Show one big number, one sentence and a small chart.
- Charts use Ink for the main series, TUFF Red for the member or "you" figure, and Stone for context.
- Always date data and name the sample size.

### 8.4 Layout

- 8px spacing grid; 12-column web grid; generous white space.
- Corner radius: 12px on cards and buttons, 4px on inputs and tags.
- Flat surfaces with a 1px Stone border at 20% opacity; avoid drop shadows.
- Each page or slide has one red moment, usually the main action or key number.

### 8.5 Motion

- The U draws itself and the two dots drop into place on load (400ms, ease-out); this is the brand's signature movement.
- Numbers count up once when they enter the screen.
- Keep everything else subtle: 150 to 200ms fades and slides. Respect reduced-motion settings.
