RE: ADHERENCE TO THE CODE OF CONDUCT FOR LEASING OF RETAIL PREMISES IN SINGAPORE

Dear Sir / Madam,

SGTUFF Co-operative Ltd writes to you on behalf of our collective membership comprising 700+ verified frontline business owners, representing over 600 commercial brands across 2,600+ retail and food & beverage outlets occupying more than 3.5 million square feet of commercial space in Singapore.

As an active constituent organization within the Fair Tenancy Industry Committee (FTIC), SGTUFF remains firmly committed to upholding the industry-led Code of Conduct for Leasing of Retail Premises. We urge all commercial leasing partners to align negotiation practices with the foundational pillars of transparency, reciprocity, and long-term sustainability.

Our secretariat stands ready to engage in constructive dialogue, lease mediation, and joint industry initiatives to support the mutual vibrancy and economic resilience of Singapore's retail and lifestyle landscape.
