The exhaustive corporate identity system, clear-space specifications, digital asset suite, and statutory reproduction rules for Singapore Tenants United for Fairness.

1. The Official Master Logo System

Primary Master Wordmark (Full Horizontal Lockup)

Clear Space Rule (1X)

Maintain an exclusion zone around the logo equal to the height of the letter "S" (denoted as X). No text, borders, or graphics may encroach.

Minimum Digital Size

140px width on standard 72/96 DPI displays. Never render smaller to preserve legibility of "SINGAPORE TENANTS UNITED FOR FAIRNESS".

Minimum Print Size

38mm width at 300 DPI on physical stationery, name cards, and formal court/mediation documents.

Strict Restrictions

Never stretch, skew, change the red hue to arbitrary colors, add heavy drop shadows, or place on unapproved low-contrast patterns.
