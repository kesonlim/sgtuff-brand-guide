Who we are

SGTUFF Co-operative Ltd is a registered co-operative society. Business owners founded it in February 2020 to speak with one voice on rent and leases.

Fair tenancy

SGTUFF Chairman Terence Yow serves on the Fair Tenancy Industry Committee. We push for fair, balanced lease terms between landlords and tenants.
